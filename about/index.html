Who we are

A fully integrated services & products business.

Bolide Tanzania — formerly Dartcom Tanzania — is a specialist telecommunications infrastructure and energy business delivering fibre, power and intelligent security across the United Republic of Tanzania.

We are a single, fully integrated partner: we distribute, build, maintain and operate — combining world-class products with the field teams and 24/7 network operations to keep them running. Headquartered in Dar es Salaam and backed by the Bolide Group, we bring global standards to local delivery.

Why clients stay

Proactive protection, real-time visibility.

Network protection — proactive, not reactive

  • Dedicated patrolling — GPS-tracked patrollers on defined routes with documented, real-time evidence.
  • Reinspection programme — every temporary repair tracked and reinspected, breaking the repeat-fault cycle.
  • Community engagement — 80+ active engagements and third-party construction monitoring.
  • Prevention over response — each prevented fault saves roughly 4 hours of service disruption. Fewer faults, not just faster fixes.
